# DESIGN-51 · Cart components and placement conform to wireframe

**Status** · Fail  
**Requirement** · Screen 5.1 Cart  
**Defect** · WC-010  
**Severity** · S4 Minor  
**Environment** · Assigned WholeCart demo, 14 November 2026

## Reproduce

**Data and setup** · Buyer cart with one product line

Open Cart and compare product, unit, unit price, quantity, line total, net total, and Proceed button with the screen 5.1 wireframe.

## Expected

Show the specified 1–20 quantity dropdown, line/net totals, and Proceed button in the wireframe layout.

## Observed

Product, unit, unit price, line total, and net total are present. Quantity is a textbox with Update; net total and Proceed are left-aligned rather than at the wireframe's lower right.

Quantity control and summary/button placement differ from the design; see WC-010.
